ELEVENWARD — PRESS FACT SHEET

Developer and publisher: Howeth Studio
Website: https://howethstudio.com/elevenward/
Press contact: howethstudio@gmail.com

Genre: Global football career and life RPG
Platforms: iOS and Android
Players: Single-player
Audience: Ages 13+
Launch languages: English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, and French

Elevenward follows one original player from prospect to retirement. Each matchweek asks the player to choose a training focus, manage risk in a decisive football moment, and live with transparent consequences across form, fitness, trust, money, relationships, contracts, transfers, trophies, and legacy.

The full career is playable offline. Optional accounts add cloud synchronization, purchase recovery, and prize-free leaderboards. The game contains no advertising, loot boxes, randomized purchases, energy timers, or pay-to-win mechanics. Optional purchases are permanent career-slot and presentation-cosmetic upgrades.

Key launch scope:
- 20-season career lifecycle
- 120 original clubs across 12 leagues in six football nations
- Promotion and relegation, domestic cups, international club competition, and a four-year national-team tournament
- Four position families and 12 archetypes
- 160 localized match situations, 200 career events, and 120 lifestyle items
- Deterministic simulation with explainable previews and post-match receipts

Brand line: The work before the noise.

All club, competition, player, and brand identities in Elevenward are original fictional creations.
